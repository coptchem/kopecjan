## Who Jan is

Jan has been into computers and IT his whole life, since primary school. He loves new technology: he enjoys learning it and then putting it to use, for himself and for others, at work and in everyday life. "No" is not an answer for him; he always looks for a way to get to the result. He is most motivated by obstacles and problems that look unsolvable at first sight, and his biggest satisfaction is solving one in the end. It does not matter to him whether it is a work project or helping family, friends or acquaintances.

## What Jan wants next

Jan has worked in IT since 2009. In recent years he has focused mainly on his own projects and his clients. Now he would like to become part of a larger team again and work on challenging projects. He always thinks about an assignment as a whole, and he has to be satisfied with the result himself. He wants to bring this approach to his next role.
