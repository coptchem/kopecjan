### Q: Is Jan open to new roles right now, and from when?

A: Yes, Jan is open to new opportunities. He can start immediately or within one month.

### Q: What employment form does Jan prefer?

A: Both a full-time employment contract and a contract via his own company (IČO, B2B) work for him. If he has to choose, he prefers full-time employment.

### Q: Does Jan work remote only?

A: Jan prefers remote work. He is happy to come to the office on site 2 to 3 times a month.

### Q: What time zone does Jan work in?

A: Jan works in CET (Central European Time) during usual business hours. He can adjust his hours to overlap with UK or US time zones.

### Q: What are Jan's rate or salary expectations?

A: Shared on request. It always depends on the specific offer.

### Q: Which roles is Jan looking for?

A: AI engineer, full-stack developer, automation engineer, solution engineer, solution architect, product owner or data analyst.

### Q: Is Jan a fit for a remote AI engineer role?

A: Jan works with AI tools every day: Claude Code by Anthropic, ChatGPT and Google Gemini. He builds agents mainly with Claude Code. He does not leave the decisions to AI alone: he relies on his many years of experience, always keeps AI under control and takes full responsibility for the result.

Examples of what he has built with AI:

- the RAG assistant "Ask about Jan" on this website, which answers questions about Jan only from his own documents,
- a system for data consistency checks and data extraction for the Flatter project (flatter.cz),
- automated processing of PDF documents from the Czech insolvency register (ISIR) for a law firm,
- automated daily scraping and processing of retail chains' promotional flyers for levne.pitpivo.cz (the Pivo v akci project).

### Q: Can Jan share code samples or references?

A: Yes. His public code is on GitHub: https://github.com/coptchem (for example the source code of this website and its assistant). Live projects and client websites he built: https://www.flatter.cz/, https://pitpivo.cz/, https://levne.pitpivo.cz/, https://tisknive3d.cz/ and https://m-eng.cz/.
