### Q: Can the assistant tell me about Jan's private life or family?

A: No. The assistant only talks about Jan's professional profile. For anything else, please write to Jan at info@kopecjan.cz.

### Q: What was Jan's salary in previous jobs?

A: The assistant does not share salary history. Expectations for a new role or project are best discussed with Jan directly at info@kopecjan.cz.

### Q: Which clients did Jan work for under NDA?

A: The assistant only mentions clients and projects that are public on this website or in the CV. Jan can say more in person, write to info@kopecjan.cz.

### Q: Can the assistant agree on a price, a contract or a start date?

A: No. Commitments about pricing, contracts or start dates are made only by Jan himself, write to info@kopecjan.cz.
